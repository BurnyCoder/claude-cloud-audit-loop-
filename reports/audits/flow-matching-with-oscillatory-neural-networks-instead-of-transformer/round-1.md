# Fact-check audit: BurnyCoder/flow-matching-with-oscillatory-neural-networks-instead-of-transformer, round 1
## Summary

The repository's claims are accurate. I parsed the JSON evidence embedded in all 83 `reports/experiments/*.md` records with a script and recomputed every headline number. That covers the six-seed mean KIDs (0.18567 / 0.09179 / 0.13487), the paired difference +0.09388, the t interval [0.08543, 0.10234], and the exact sign-flip p = 0.03125 (all 6 of 6 differences are positive, so p is the minimum possible 2/64). It also covers the FID, throughput, latency and peak-memory means, the 0.504041 and 2.14506 ratios, the ablation and intervention deltas, the seed-101 NFE curve, the run and evaluation counts (30 and 43), and the training durations (2,700.0286 to 2,700.2957 s).

Method claims match the code and the saved configs: AdamW with lr 3e-4, betas (0.9, 0.999), weight decay 0.01, batch 128 with microbatch 32, gradient clipping at 1.0, EMA 0.999. The models use width 128, 4 blocks, 4x4 patches, 4-D oscillators, 4 inner steps of size 0.1, a midpoint solver at 32 NFE, and DiT ffn 576. Parameter counts are 1,297,712, 1,298,864 and 1,296,688, and the ablation counts differ by exactly the coupling and frequency weights. The paper's equation matches AKOrN equations 4–7 on the arXiv HTML v2 page. All 7 cited arXiv papers have the stated titles, authors and IDs. All 64 external URLs resolve (the GitHub repositories were confirmed through package metadata or git). The cited commits exist, and the CI runs and their test counts (27, 35, 36) match the claims. The local suite has 37 tests, as claimed.

Provenance claims also check out against the public Hub artifacts: budget.json shows 67,451.7 charged seconds, or 18.7366 h. The freeze timestamp and revision match. The split counts are 45,000 / 5,000 / 10,000 with no removals. The recorded environment is torch 2.11.0+cu128 on an RTX 5070 Laptop GPU.

I found no wrong statements. My only fixes add missing primary sources and complete a library list.
